Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with many layers.
It has revolutionized fields like computer vision, natural language processing, and speech recognition.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are used for image classification tasks.
They use filters to detect edges, shapes, and textures in images.
CNNs consist of convolution layers, pooling layers, and fully connected layers.

Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are designed for sequential data like text and time series.
They maintain a hidden state that captures information from previous steps.
LSTM and GRU are improved versions of RNNs that solve the vanishing gradient problem.

Transfer learning allows models trained on large datasets to be reused for new tasks.
This reduces training time and improves performance with limited data.
Popular pre-trained models include VGG, ResNet, BERT, and GPT.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines retrieval systems with language models.
It retrieves relevant documents first and then generates answers grounded in those documents.
RAG improves factual accuracy and works well for private or domain-specific data.
